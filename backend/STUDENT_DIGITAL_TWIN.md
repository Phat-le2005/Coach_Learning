# Student Digital Twin

`StudentDigitalTwinService` builds a transient read model from normalized relational tables. The snapshot is not persisted, cached, generated by an LLM, or treated as a separate source of truth.

## Sources

- basic profile: `users` and `student_profiles`;
- curriculum progress: Subject, Chapter, Topic, active ProblemType, and problem-type mastery;
- knowledge state: Concept mastery, direct prerequisite relations, and global ProblemType–Concept mappings;
- recent activity: `practice_sessions`, `question_attempts`, and Question mappings;
- review due: the current derived mastery state;
- study behavior: aggregate counts and timing from official Practice Engine tables.

The snapshot deliberately keeps ProblemType and Concept signals separate. A weak `PARAMETER_MONOTONICITY` ProblemType and a weak `INEQUALITY_SOLVING` Concept appear in different collections. `problem_type_concept_links` preserves the global curriculum mapping between them without claiming that the Concept caused the weakness.

AcademicGoal, misconception, study-plan, exam-performance, and risk-indicator fields are omitted because those domains do not exist yet. Synthetic legacy `student_attempts` are not mixed into official Practice Engine activity.

## Snapshot

The snapshot contains:

- `basic_profile`;
- `curriculum_progress` with Subject → Chapter → Topic → ProblemType mastery;
- `knowledge_state` with Concept mastery, prerequisites, and ProblemType–Concept links;
- strong and weak ProblemTypes;
- strong and weak Concepts;
- up to 20 recent attempts;
- review-due Concepts and ProblemTypes;
- derived study behavior.

All hierarchy/mastery tables are loaded in bounded batches. Recent Question mappings are also loaded in two batch queries. No query runs once per mastery row.

## AI context

`get_ai_context(student_id, context_type)` supports:

- `TUTOR`: weaknesses, relevant ProblemType–Concept links, review due, and five recent attempts;
- `PLANNER`: grade, subject summaries, weaknesses, review due, and study behavior;
- `TEACHER_COPILOT`: profile, summaries, filtered strengths/weaknesses, activity, and mapping context;
- `PARENT_SUMMARY`: name, grade, subject summaries, plain strength/support labels, and study behavior;
- `EXAM_ANALYSIS`: subject summaries, weak ProblemTypes, and only attempts from `EXAM` sessions.

These methods return structured data only and do not call an AI provider.

## API

- `GET /students/me/digital-twin`
- `GET /teacher/students/{student_id}/digital-twin`

Teacher access requires an assignment to a classroom where the student has an `ACTIVE` enrollment. Parent access is not exposed.

No migration is required for this read model. The database remains at the existing Alembic head.
